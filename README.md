# System Thinking and Mapping Outcomes – Spotify India: Increasing Time Spent Listening

## Overview

This is a product case study on Spotify India. The goal is to understand how Spotify can increase the time users spend listening.

## What I looked at

- India’s music streaming market
- Key competitors and how they compete for listeners
- Spotify’s listening experience and features
- Why time spent listening matters
- KPI breakdown of time spent listening
- Customer segmentation
- Product outcomes that could increase listening time

## Key takeaway

The analysis focuses on increasing **Listening Days per User** among **lower-frequency Free users**.

Other possible outcomes include:

- More sessions per listening day
- Longer average listening time per session

## Framework used

**Business outcome → KPI breakdown → Customer segment → Product outcome**
